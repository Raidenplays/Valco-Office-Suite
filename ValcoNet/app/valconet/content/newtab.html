      InPrivate

      InPrivate is turned on

      When InPrivate Browsing is turned on, you will see this indicator in the address bar and the title bar.

      InPrivate Browsing helps prevent ValcoNet from storing data about your browsing session. This includes cookies, temporary Internet files, history, and other data. Toolbars and extensions are disabled unless you've allowed them in private windows.

      To turn off InPrivate Browsing, close this browser window.
